About Laura Jorden, Compass Luxury Real Estate Agent

Scottsdale & Paradise Valley Luxury Real Estate Data-Driven Advisor Relocation • Investment • Strategic Guidance

Laura Jorden is dedicated to providing a five-star real estate experience, whether it's a first-time townhome purchase, a family home, or an investment opportunity.

As an Arizona resident of more than 35 years with a background in chemistry and economics, Laura blends data-driven market insight with sharp analysis. She listens closely to each client's goals, then works to secure or sell the right property to meet them.

Drawing on her own experience raising a family and investing in real estate, Laura offers clients practical, first-hand support beyond the transaction itself. Her work ethic and continual pursuit of professional growth are reflected in the level of service she provides.

Laura has been a licensed Arizona REALTOR® since 2005 and has been recognized nationally by Real Trends and The Wall Street Journal as a top 1.5% REALTOR® in America. She specializes in Paradise Valley, Scottsdale, Fountain Hills, the East Valley, and Phoenix, with an additional focus on equestrian properties, and is an active member of the Luxury Home Tour. She holds Chemistry and Economics degrees with a minor in Biology from Indiana University, and beyond real estate she has served as a board member for Scottsdale Unified Schools (Sequoya and Cocopah) and for Phoenix Rising, along with time as a team manager for Phoenix Rising and SC Del Sol Soccer Club.

Outside the Office

What Does Laura Jorden Do Outside of Real Estate?

Outside of real estate, Laura spends time with her family, hikes and golfs across Arizona, and cheers on the Indiana University Hoosiers — a loyalty that traces back to her Midwest upbringing and her chemistry and economics studies at IU. That blend of Midwest roots and 30-plus years in Arizona shapes the neighborhood guidance she gives clients — from trailhead access to golf course communities to which streets are walkable to dinner.
