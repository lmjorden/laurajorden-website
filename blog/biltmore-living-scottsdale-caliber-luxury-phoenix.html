Biltmore Living: Scottsdale-Caliber Luxury Minutes from Downtown Phoenix

By Laura Jorden, Compass  ·  Published October 10, 2026

What is the Biltmore area, and why is it considered one of Phoenix's premier luxury enclaves?

Often called the "Jewel of the Desert," the Biltmore corridor sits between 24th and 32nd Street, just north of Camelback Road, anchored by the historic Arizona Biltmore Hotel and two manicured 18-hole golf courses — the Tom Lehman-designed Estates Course and the Links Course. Set against the backdrop of Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak, it's one of the few Phoenix neighborhoods that combines genuine golf-resort scenery with an urban, walkable core.

How does Biltmore compare to Scottsdale for luxury buyers?

The finishes, amenities, and golf pedigree in Biltmore are on par with what buyers expect in Scottsdale, but the location is more central — Biltmore residents typically have a shorter commute to downtown Phoenix offices and to Sky Harbor International Airport than they would from North Scottsdale. For buyers who split their time between Phoenix and travel, or who work downtown, that difference in daily drive time is often the deciding factor over an otherwise similar Scottsdale property.

What kind of homes are available in Biltmore?

Biltmore's housing stock is more architecturally diverse than much of Scottsdale, ranging from luxury high-rise and mid-rise condominiums to fully custom estates. That mix makes it a strong option both for buyers who want a lock-and-leave condo with resort-style amenities and for those who want a larger custom home without leaving the corridor's walkable core.

What's the day-to-day lifestyle like?

Biltmore Fashion Park anchors the area's shopping and dining, and the broader Camelback Corridor adds a concentration of upscale restaurants, offices, and healthcare within easy reach. Combined with the golf courses, spa access through the Arizona Biltmore Hotel, and an active nightlife scene, Biltmore offers a genuinely urban version of the Scottsdale resort lifestyle.

Who is the ideal Biltmore buyer?

Biltmore tends to fit buyers who want proximity to downtown Phoenix and the airport without giving up golf-course views and resort amenities, executives who split time between cities, and buyers who prefer a low-maintenance condo lifestyle over a larger single-family lot. It's also a strong fit for seasonal residents who want walkability to dining and shopping alongside the resort feel, without the drive time North Scottsdale requires.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Is Biltmore in Phoenix or Scottsdale?

Biltmore is in Phoenix, located between 24th and 32nd Street just north of Camelback Road — close enough to Scottsdale to share its luxury market, but with a more central, urban location.

What golf courses are in the Biltmore area?

The Arizona Biltmore Golf Club features two 18-hole courses: the Tom Lehman-designed Estates Course and the Links Course, both within the neighborhood.

Are there condos available in Biltmore, or only single-family homes?

Both. Biltmore's housing stock ranges from luxury high-rise and mid-rise condominiums to custom single-family estates, giving buyers more architectural variety than many single-family-dominated Scottsdale communities.

How far is Biltmore from Scottsdale and Sky Harbor Airport?

Biltmore sits between Phoenix and Scottsdale, with a shorter typical commute to downtown Phoenix and Sky Harbor International Airport than most North Scottsdale communities.

Is Biltmore a good fit for buyers who want a walkable, resort-style lifestyle?

Yes. Biltmore Fashion Park and the Camelback Corridor put shopping, dining, and golf within easy reach, giving buyers a walkable, resort-caliber lifestyle in a more central location than North Scottsdale.
