Inside Scottsdale's Guard-Gated Communities: Desert Mountain, Silverleaf & Troon North Compared

By Laura Jorden, Compass  ·  Published October 3, 2026

What actually makes a community "guard-gated" versus simply gated?

A gated community can mean nothing more than a keypad or remote-controlled arm at the entrance. Guard-gated means a live, staffed gatehouse controlling every entry, which is the standard buyers expect — and get — in communities like Desert Mountain and Silverleaf. For buyers whose top priority is privacy and security rather than golf access alone, that distinction matters more than almost anything else on the amenity list.

How does Desert Mountain compare to the others?

Desert Mountain is built around golf at a scale nothing else in the Valley matches: six Jack Nicklaus Signature courses, spread across multiple clubhouses, along with tennis, extensive hiking trails, and full spa services. It's the right fit for a buyer who genuinely wants variety in their golf and a resort campus large enough that no two rounds feel the same. The tradeoff is that Desert Mountain sits further from central Scottsdale than Silverleaf or Troon North, so it suits buyers who want deep desert seclusion more than quick access to Old Town.

What's distinct about Silverleaf?

Silverleaf pairs custom estate architecture with a private Tom Weiskopf-designed golf course and an elegant clubhouse and spa. As part of the larger DC Ranch master association, Silverleaf residents also get access to the Desert Camp and Homestead community centers and DC Ranch's trail system on top of their own amenities. Full golf membership is a serious commitment — as of mid-2026, initiation runs around $500,000 (non-equity, non-refundable) with monthly dues in the $2,750–$3,900 range — so it's worth confirming current membership terms directly with the club before you fall in love with a lot.

What's distinct about Troon North?

Troon North is known for its dramatic boulder formations and desert scenery as much as its golf, with two championship courses and the private Troon Country Club, designed by Jay Morrish and Tom Weiskopf. It offers a similar caliber of course design to Silverleaf and Desert Mountain but with a broader range of price points, which makes it a common landing spot for buyers who want the guard-gated golf lifestyle without necessarily competing at the very top of the market.

How should a buyer decide between them?

Start with how central golf is to your daily life: if you want maximum course variety, Desert Mountain's six courses are hard to match. If you want custom-estate architecture and don't mind a premium membership structure, Silverleaf is the benchmark. If you want championship-caliber golf with more flexibility on price point and a slightly shorter drive to Scottsdale's restaurants and shopping, Troon North is usually the better fit. I walk buyers through current membership costs, HOA structures, and resale trends for each before we start touring, since those details vary community to community and change over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

What's the difference between "gated" and "guard-gated" in Scottsdale?

A gated community may only have a keypad or remote-controlled entry, while guard-gated means a staffed gatehouse controls access at all times. Desert Mountain and Silverleaf are both guard-gated, which is a major part of their appeal for privacy-focused buyers.

Which is the most exclusive: Desert Mountain, Silverleaf, or Troon North?

Desert Mountain and Silverleaf are generally considered the most exclusive, both guard-gated with award-winning golf and some of the Valley's highest price points. Troon North offers a similar caliber of golf with a broader range of price points.

Do all three require golf club membership to buy a home?

Membership structure varies by community and can change over time, so it's important to confirm current requirements and costs directly with each club. Some bundle certain access into HOA dues, while full golf membership elsewhere involves a separate initiation fee and monthly dues.

Which community has the most golf courses?

Desert Mountain, with six Jack Nicklaus Signature courses across multiple clubhouses. Silverleaf and Troon North each offer fewer courses but comparable design pedigree.

Are home prices similar across all three communities?

No. Silverleaf and Desert Mountain tend to sit at the top of the North Scottsdale market, while Troon North offers a wider range of price points, making it accessible to a broader set of buyers while still delivering guard-gated, championship-golf living.
