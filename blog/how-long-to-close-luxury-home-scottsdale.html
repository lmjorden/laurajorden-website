How Long Does It Take to Close on a Luxury Home in Scottsdale?

By Laura Jorden, Compass  ·  Published June 18, 2026

How long does it take to close on a luxury home in Scottsdale?

A luxury home purchase in Scottsdale typically closes in 30 to 45 days with conventional or jumbo financing, and in as little as 7 to 14 days for a cash purchase. [VERIFY current timelines with escrow/title company, as they vary by lender and property] The exact timeline depends on financing type, whether an appraisal is required, and how quickly inspection contingencies are resolved.

What is the typical closing timeline for a financed purchase?

For a jumbo or conventional loan, the timeline generally includes an initial inspection period of about 10 days under Arizona's standard purchase contract, followed by loan underwriting, appraisal, and title work that together take another three to five weeks. [VERIFY current contract terms and lender timelines]

Jumbo loans, which are common for Scottsdale and Paradise Valley purchases above roughly $800,000 to $1 million, can take longer to underwrite than conforming loans because lenders apply additional documentation and reserve requirements. [VERIFY current jumbo loan thresholds and requirements with a lender]

What is the typical closing timeline for a cash purchase?

Cash purchases can close in as little as 7 to 14 days, since there is no loan underwriting or lender-required appraisal to wait on. Even in a cash transaction, most buyers still choose to complete a home inspection and, for unique luxury properties, a private appraisal to confirm value.

Why do luxury home appraisals sometimes take longer?

Luxury and custom homes in areas like Paradise Valley or the Cactus Corridor can be harder to appraise because there are fewer directly comparable recent sales, especially for one-of-a-kind architecture on large lots. Appraisers may need additional time to identify appropriate comps, which can extend the overall timeline by several days to a couple of weeks. [VERIFY with a local appraiser for a specific property]

What can delay closing on a luxury home?

The most common delays involve financing (incomplete lender documentation or a low appraisal), inspection negotiations on older or custom-built homes, and title issues on properties with private wells, easements, or no HOA record-keeping, which is common in areas like the Cactus Corridor. [VERIFY specific title issues with an escrow officer before writing an offer]

Working with a lender experienced in jumbo loans and an escrow team familiar with luxury and estate-lot properties typically reduces the risk of last-minute delays.

How can buyers and sellers keep a luxury closing on schedule?

Getting fully underwritten (not just pre-qualified) before writing an offer is the single biggest factor in keeping a financed luxury purchase on schedule, since it removes most financing-related delay risk. Sellers can help by having survey, well, septic, or HOA documentation ready in advance for properties where that paperwork isn't centrally recorded.

Common Questions

How long does it take to close on a luxury home in Scottsdale?

A financed luxury home purchase in Scottsdale typically closes in 30 to 45 days, while a cash purchase can close in as little as 7 to 14 days. Exact timelines vary by lender, property type, and how quickly contingencies are resolved.

Do jumbo loans take longer to close than conventional loans?

Often, yes. Jumbo loans, which are common for Scottsdale and Paradise Valley purchases above roughly $800,000 to $1 million, typically involve additional underwriting documentation and can take longer than a conforming conventional loan.

Can you close on a home in Arizona in less than two weeks?

Yes, primarily with a cash purchase. A cash transaction can close in as little as 7 to 14 days since there is no lender underwriting or required appraisal to wait on.

Why do custom luxury homes sometimes take longer to appraise?

Custom and estate-lot homes in areas like Paradise Valley or the Cactus Corridor can be harder to appraise because there are fewer directly comparable recent sales, which can add several days to a couple of weeks to the appraisal process.

What is the standard inspection period in an Arizona purchase contract?

Arizona's standard residential purchase contract typically includes an inspection period of about 10 days, though this can be negotiated between buyer and seller. Buyers should confirm current contract terms with their agent before relying on this figure.
