What do the November numbers mean for luxury buyers?

Look at months of supply and days on market together. Where inventory is deeper and homes are taking longer to sell, there is usually more room to negotiate on price, repairs, or closing timeline. Where homes are moving quickly near full price, preparation matters more: have financing or proof of funds ready before you tour.

Fall is when many seasonal residents return to the Valley, and plenty of luxury buyers tour while they are here. A short list of neighborhoods and a clear budget will make each visit count. What $2M+ Buys You in Scottsdale is a good neighborhood-by-neighborhood starting point.

What do the November numbers mean for luxury sellers?

Price to the market the numbers describe, not the one you remember. A home priced in line with recent closed sales tends to draw its strongest interest in the first few weeks on the market; an overpriced home can sit, invite lower offers, and leave money on the table. Preparation, photography, and timing still matter at every price point.
