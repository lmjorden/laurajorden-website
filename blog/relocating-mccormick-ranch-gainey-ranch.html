Relocating to McCormick Ranch & Gainey Ranch: A Buyer's Guide

By Laura Jorden, Compass  ·  Published June 25, 2026

What should I know before relocating to McCormick Ranch or Gainey Ranch?

McCormick Ranch and Gainey Ranch are master-planned Scottsdale communities built around lakes, golf courses, and greenbelts, with home prices ranging from $450,000 condos to $4 million-plus golf course estates. Relocating buyers should decide early whether they want the broader, more affordable mix of McCormick Ranch or the more resort-oriented, guard-gated feel of Gainey Ranch.

Step 1: Which part of the community fits your budget?

Home TypeTypical Price
Condominiums$450,000–$800,000
Single-family homes$900,000–$1.8M
Golf course homes$1.5M–$4M+

Relocating buyers coming from outside Arizona should budget for golf course or lakefront frontage as a separate premium on top of base square footage, since comparable floor plans can vary significantly in price based on view and water access.

Step 2: Why get pre-approved with an Arizona-familiar lender first?

Because much of the inventory in this area is condos and golf villas within an HOA, lenders will need current HOA financials and reserve information as part of underwriting. Working with a lender familiar with Arizona HOA and condo requirements can prevent delays. [VERIFY current lender/HOA documentation requirements]

Step 3: Why tour communities in person instead of relying on photos?

McCormick Ranch and Gainey Ranch each include multiple distinct villages and communities with different HOA rules, price points, and proximity to the Scottsdale Greenbelt. A property that looks similar in photos can feel very different in person depending on its distance from a lake, golf course, or the greenbelt's bike path network.

Step 4: What HOA documents should you review before writing an offer?

Most communities in this area carry an HOA that maintains shared greenbelt, lake, and golf-adjacent landscaping. Relocating buyers should request the HOA's CC&Rs, current dues, and reserve study before writing an offer, since dues and rules vary meaningfully by community. [VERIFY current HOA dues per community]

Step 5: When is the best time to plan a move to Scottsdale?

Scottsdale's real estate market and moving-service availability are typically busiest during the winter and early spring season, when seasonal residents are most active. Relocating buyers with flexibility may find smoother scheduling for inspections, movers, and closing appointments outside of that peak window. [VERIFY current seasonal market conditions]

Step 6: Should you start in McCormick Ranch or Gainey Ranch?

Rather than touring both communities broadly, relocating buyers save time by narrowing their search first: McCormick Ranch for a wider range of price points and home styles across multiple villages, or Gainey Ranch for a smaller, more resort-oriented footprint built around guard-gated golf villa communities.

Once narrowed, request a list of recent closed sales — not just active listings — in the specific village or community you're considering, since pricing can vary meaningfully between villages within the same master plan.

What should out-of-state buyers know before relocating here?

Arizona does not require an attorney at closing, and escrow is typically handled by a title company rather than an attorney's office, which can be a different process than in many other states. [VERIFY current Arizona closing process with your escrow officer] Out-of-state buyers should also budget time to tour in person during Scottsdale's late spring or summer heat if a winter visit isn't possible, since the climate experience differs significantly by season.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Is McCormick Ranch or Gainey Ranch better for relocating buyers?

It depends on budget and priorities. McCormick Ranch offers a broader range of price points and home styles across multiple villages, while Gainey Ranch is smaller and more resort-oriented, built around guard-gated golf villa communities.

Do I need to join a golf club to live in McCormick Ranch or Gainey Ranch?

No. Golf course frontage and club membership are typically separate; owning a home near or backing a golf course does not automatically include club membership. Buyers should confirm membership requirements and costs for a specific community. [VERIFY]

What HOA documents should I review before buying?

Request the HOA's CC&Rs, current monthly or annual dues, and the most recent reserve study before writing an offer, since these vary by specific community within McCormick Ranch and Gainey Ranch.

How walkable is McCormick Ranch to the Scottsdale Greenbelt?

Many McCormick Ranch and Gainey Ranch neighborhoods have direct or near-direct access to the Scottsdale Greenbelt's connected bike and walking paths, though exact proximity varies by specific street and community.
