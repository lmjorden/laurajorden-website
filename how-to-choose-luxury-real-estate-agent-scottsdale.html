How to Choose a Luxury Real Estate Agent in Scottsdale

The questions worth asking before you hire anyone to buy or sell a luxury home, and why it matters who actually handles your transaction.

What questions should you ask a luxury agent before hiring?

  1. What have you sold near me recently? Ask for closed sales in your neighborhood and price range from the past year, not just active listings.
  2. Who will actually handle my sale or purchase? Find out who prices the home, who shows it, who negotiates, and who answers your calls on a Sunday night.
  3. How will you price my home? A strong answer walks you through closed sales, homes that didn't sell, and current competition, not just a number.
  4. What is your marketing plan? Ask about photography, video, pre-marketing options, and how the agent reaches buyers relocating from out of state.
  5. How do you negotiate? Ask for an example of a recent negotiation, including what was given up and what was gained.
  6. How will we communicate? Agree on how often you'll hear from the agent and how quickly they respond.
  7. Can I verify your track record? Independent rankings such as RealTrends publish verified sales volume for agents.

Solo agent or team: why does it matter?

Many luxury agents run teams. A team can cover more showings at once, but the agent who wins your listing may not be the person who negotiates your offer or answers your texts. A solo agent means one point of contact who knows your home, your goals, and your bottom line at every step.

Neither model is right for everyone. What matters is knowing which one you're hiring, so ask directly and get the answer in writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

What should I look for in a luxury real estate agent in Scottsdale?

Look for recent sales in your neighborhood and price range, a clear pricing strategy backed by data, a specific marketing plan, strong negotiation experience, and clarity about who will personally handle your transaction.

Is it better to hire a solo agent or a real estate team?

It depends on what you value. Teams can offer more hands for showings and paperwork, while a solo agent means the person you hire is the person who prices, markets, negotiates, and answers your calls. Ask any agent exactly who will handle each step.

How can I verify an agent's sales record?

Ask for recent closed sales in your area, and check independent sources such as RealTrends, which publishes verified agent rankings by sales volume.
