Who Is Laura Jorden?

Laura Jorden has lived in Arizona for more than 35 years and has been a full-time Compass Real Estate Agent serving Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, and Phoenix. She brings a background in chemistry and economics to every transaction, pairing data-driven market analysis with hands-on negotiation.

Laura works with first-time buyers, families, and investors, and she is equally comfortable with a townhome purchase, a family home search, or a multi-property investment strategy.
